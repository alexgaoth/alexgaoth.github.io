claude-inboxes

A personal tool that lets Alex Gao's own AI assistant (Claude Code) search, read and draft email in his own Gmail accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

The app reads Gmail and creates drafts only for the account owner who signs in. Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Email content is passed to the owner's AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) only when the owner asks it to look at mail. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used to train AI models. Use of data from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
